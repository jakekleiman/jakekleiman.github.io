## Projects

### [Lineup Lab: Grading How MLB Teams Order Their Lineups](/lineup-lab/)

An R Shiny app that takes the nine starting hitters from any 2026 MLB game and shows how much a different batting order would change expected runs. I used it to grade how well all 30 teams ordered their lineups.
